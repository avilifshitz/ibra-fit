How it works.
Ten steps.

From your profile to next week. The first four happen once. The rest, every week. The last two are Pro only.

In short: pick a goal · IBRA builds a plan for your gear · train and log · close the week, and IBRA progresses your plan. In Pro: share the week with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and it fine-tunes.

The app screens below are from the Hebrew edition.

Once
1

Your profile.

Name, nickname, age, height. Weight and body fat if you know them. Whatever Apple Health already knows is read with your permission and stays on your phone.

2

Your gear.

Tick it from the list or type it in. For every weight: the range and the increments. No pull-up bar? The plan is built without one.

3

Your target rank.

Son of Valor, Man of Valor or Mighty Man of Valor — five conditions each. You pick the target. The rank you've actually reached is set at your weekly closes.

4

The plan.

IBRA builds a plan for your gear, your time and your goal: workouts, sets, reps and weights. No chat, no copy-paste. You can train the same day.

In Pro you can build it with the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini you already use: IBRA writes it one document, and the reply goes straight into the app.

Every week
5

The workout.

“Today” tells you what's next. In each set: the weight and reps from last time, and what this week needs. The rest timer runs on its own. At the end — what you lifted, and what improved.

6

Food and measurements.

A meal is ticked off with one tap; whatever you didn't eat rolls into the next meal, in grams. Weight every morning if the plan asks, waist every week, shoulders every three.

7

The photos.

Every three weeks: same poses, same spot, same light. The frame shows your last photo as a ghost so you stand the same way. In Pro, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini tells you what it saw in each pose. Your photos never leave your phone unless you send them.

8

The weekly close.

On the weekend. You measure what the week asks for, and IBRA progresses your plan based on what you did: a little more weight, a rep or a set, exactly when you're ready.

In Pro: with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini
9

Sharing.

IBRA packs the week: workouts, your top set for every exercise, weight, waist, sleep and photos. “Share” sends the package to your chat. You see exactly what's sent before it goes. When the coach replies, you bring the answer back to IBRA.

10

Approving the changes.

Paste the reply. IBRA shows only what changes — and why. You approve, and next week starts from there.

You do the work. IBRA keeps you on track.
