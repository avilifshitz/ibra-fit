What IBRA does with the plan, and what it doesn't.

The plan and the AI

What does IBRA do?

You pick the body you want, and IBRA builds the way there: a plan for the gear you have, workouts set by set, measurements, and at the end of every week it progresses your plan based on what you did. Free, for every man. In Pro it connects to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, which also see your photos and fine-tune every week.

How do I move a workout plan from ChatGPT into the app?

In Pro, in three steps, without typing a single exercise. Without Pro, IBRA builds the plan itself.

  1. The briefing. Answer once: where you're headed, your gear, your time, injuries and what you eat. IBRA turns that into a briefing for your coach — the chat you picked (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini).
  2. The coach writes. Share the briefinging to your chat. It returns a full plan, in a format IBRA understands.
  3. Bring it back. Copy the reply, or “Share” straight from the chat to IBRA. The plan goes live: workouts, meals, measurements.

Does it work with Claude and Gemini too?

Yes. IBRA isn't tied to one provider: the briefing is text, and the reply is text. Any AI chat that can read instructions and return a structured file works. Picked a provider? You can switch any week.

How do I get ChatGPT to adjust my plan every week?

That's what the weekly close does. At the end of every week IBRA packs what really happened into one package: sets and weights, meals, weight and waist, sleep and heart rate if you allowed it, and photos every three weeks. You share it with the coach, and it sends back a list of changes. You approve, and next week starts from the updated version. Without that, every chat starts from zero.

What prompt should I give ChatGPT to build a good plan?

You don't need to write one. The briefing IBRA writes is the prompt: your gear and time, where you're starting from (measurements, the pyramid, photos), what you eat, how to log weight, and the format to send back. About 30,000 characters, written from your answers, not from scratch.

Is IBRA an AI coach?

No. IBRA builds a plan and progresses it by fixed rules, not by an AI model. If you want an AI coach, Pro connects the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini you already have: it analyzes your photos and fine-tunes the plan, while IBRA runs it, measures and writes it a summary.

Do I need a paid ChatGPT?

IBRA works without any AI. In Pro it connects to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, even on their free plans.

Day to day

What if I'm away, or short on time?

Before any workout you can pick another version of it: for the gear you have at a hotel or a park, or a shorter one when time's tight. The coach writes them ahead of time, as part of the plan.

How much work is it for me?

Once: answer a few questions. Once a week: measure and close. In Pro you also share with the chat and bring the reply back. Everything else is the workout.

What happens during the workout?

Every set shows the weight from last time and what to do now. Rest with a big timer, on the lock screen too. A demo video in one tap, without leaving the workout. The set is logged, and the next one is already worked out by the rule your plan follows.

What about food?

The meals from your plan show up on your day, to tick off and weigh, ingredient by ingredient, in grams. Your menu becomes a shopping list. Ate out? Say so, and it goes into your weekly summary.

Why photos?

Because a good plan is built from what you see, not just what you weigh. Every three weeks IBRA takes three poses with last time's silhouette as a guide, so the angle and light match and you see what changed. In Pro you share them with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini along with your measurements; it tells you what it saw in each pose and what that changes in the plan, and IBRA marks it on the photo. Your photos stay with you; the coach only gets them when you share.

What's a rank?

Son of Valor · Man of Valor · Mighty Man of Valor — from the Hebrew ben chayil, ish chayil and gibor chayil. Five measured conditions: shoulders against waist, body fat, muscle mass for your height, posture and the pyramid. Your rank is only set at a weekly close, and you can't pick it.

Does it work at a gym or outdoors?

Yes. The plan is built from the gear you ticked — at home, at a gym or outside.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The workout is complete on your phone, and heart rate and sleep come in from Apple Health from any watch. The IBRA watch app, with your set, rest and heart rate on your wrist, is part of Pro.

Price and the road

How much does it cost?

You can train with IBRA for free: a plan for your gear, workouts, the weekly close, measurements, the fitness test and the posture plan. Pro connects your AI as a coach: weekly adjustments, photo analysis, body fat, posture in degrees, charts and Apple Watch — $69.99 a year or $12.99 a month. Your first close with the coach is on us. The AI chat you pick may cost money with its provider, not with us.

What if I can't afford it?

Write to us at support@ibra.fit. We'll send you a code, no questions asked.

What's a weekly close?

At every weekly close you see what you planned against what you did, and IBRA progresses your plan. In Pro it also goes to the coach. Even a week that fell apart gets closed, and what happened is recorded. It's a fact, not a grade.

I missed my target. Now what?

Close the week, and the coach gets the real picture and adjusts next week. A bad week doesn't cancel anything. The road continues from what happened, not from what was planned.

What if I stopped completely?

When you come back, you pick up where you left off. Nothing gets locked: not the plan, not your records and not your rank.

Will I lose my rank?

Not as a punishment. Your rank only moves with your measurements, and only drops after two closes in a row measured below it.

Can I try it before I commit?

Yes. There's a sample day with no sign-up, on the first screen (“See what a day looks like”), and the workouts themselves are free. You try Pro at your first close with the coach, which is on us.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Android comes after the iPhone launch.

What happens to my data?

No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Your data lives on your phone and in your private iCloud; we don't have a copy. Sleep, heart rate and weight from Apple Health stay on the device. The coach only gets what you share, when you share it.

Do I need internet to train?

No. There's no server, so the workout runs with no signal, in a basement or a garage. Only the videos and sharing with the chat need a connection.

Does IBRA send things to the chat on its own?

No. You share, you see what's sent before it goes, and you approve the new plan. Whatever you paste or share in your chat, body photos included, is covered by the privacy policy of the provider you picked (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google), not ours. Inside IBRA everything stays on your device and in your private iCloud.

How is IBRA different from Hevy or Strong?

Hevy and Strong are excellent workout logs: you type in your plan and record your sets. IBRA starts from the other side: it builds the plan for your gear, measures your body and not just your sets, and progresses your plan every week. If you already have a plan and only want to log sets, Hevy starts free and is enough.

In Hebrew, gever (man) and gibor (hero) share the same root, G-B-R. That's why the line is “Man. As in hero.” And that's what IBRA measures: not who you are — what you've built. Rank after rank.
