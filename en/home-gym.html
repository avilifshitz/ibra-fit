A workout plan
for the gear you
already own.

Not a plan for a gym you don't have. A plan for the pull-up bar in your doorway and two dumbbells, or a barbell up to 100 kg — and for the time you have.

“I've already put money into gear and time into training. I want my work tied to a plan and a goal.”

Four steps.

  1. Set it up. What's at home, with the weight ranges. How many days, how many minutes. Injuries. And your target rank.
  2. IBRA plans. A plan for exactly your gear: every exercise, every set and every weight, matched to what you own.
  3. Train. During the workout IBRA shows your next set, counts your rest, and logs every weight and rep.
  4. Close the week. IBRA compares what you planned with what you did, and progresses your plan. In Pro you also share it with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and it fine-tunes.

The gear your plan is built on.

Tick it from the list, or type in what isn't there. For every weight — the range and the increments, so you're never told to lift 32.5 when you own 30.

  • Bands
  • Weight vest
  • Bodyweight only

No pull-up bar? The plan is built without one. Only dumbbells? That's what the plan is built from. Your gear travels with every weekly close, and an exercise you can't do with what you own is a bug, not a plan.

Already using ChatGPT?

Great — in Pro it plugs in. The problem with a chat on its own is week two: it doesn't know what you lifted, how you slept, or whether your weight moved. IBRA does, and writes it a summary you share with one tap.

And in week one too: IBRA knows your dumbbells go up in 2.5 kg increments, so the next weight is one you can actually load. In Pro, gear you haven't touched reaches the coach at the close, and if it wrote a version for a day without gear or without time, that version is waiting in your workout.
