Health and assumption of risk

Physical activity carries inherent risk. You decide what to do and you bear that risk. Intense training, fasting and changes to your diet can cause injury or make an existing condition worse. Talk to a doctor before you start, especially if you have: heart disease or high blood pressure · diabetes · a breathing problem · a back or joint injury · surgery in the last six months · an eating disorder · pregnancy or the postpartum period · you're under 18. Stop immediately and see a doctor if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or sharp pain.
