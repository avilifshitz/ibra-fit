A workout plan from ChatGPT.
How to actually run it.

ChatGPT writes a good plan in a minute. The plan isn't the problem. Week two is.

1

The chat doesn't remember what you did.

On day one everything's clear. By day eight you don't remember what you lifted last week, and ChatGPT definitely doesn't. It can't see your sets, your weight, your short nights. Without that it can write a plan, but it can't fix one.

Copying your log back into the chat by hand, every week, gets old fast.

2

Give it facts, not “build me a plan.”

A good plan is built from five things only you know, plus the shape you ask the answer to come in. Without them you get paragraphs.

  • Where to, as a number80 kg at 12% body fat, or 15 pull-ups
  • Your gear, with rangesDumbbells up to 30, barbell up to 100
  • Your timeHow many days, how many minutes a workout
  • Injuries and limitsEven “a touchy knee” changes a plan
  • What you actually eatThe proteins and carbs you like
  • And how to answerSets × reps, rest, starting weight

The minimal prompt, if you're building it yourself

I'm a 42-year-old man, 75 kg, 20% body fat, 178 cm. Goal: 80 kg at 12% body fat. Gear: barbell and plates up to 100 kg, dumbbells up to 30, pull-up bar. 4 workouts a week, up to 60 minutes. My left knee is touchy in deep squats. Food: chicken, eggs, tuna, rice, sweet potato, bread. Build a 6-week plan. For each workout: a list of exercises with sets × reps, rest in seconds, and a starting weight. No explanations. At the end of every week I'll send you what I did, and you send back only what changes.

Swap the numbers for yours. The briefing IBRA writes from your answers is the same thing, dozens of times over: with measurements, the pyramid, photos, and how to log weight.

3

Where to run the plan.

You've got a plan from the chat. Now you need to do it, set by set, and tell the chat what really happened.

  • In the chat itselfChatGPT · Claude · Gemini

    The plan lives there, and it adjusts when you tell it.

    Breaks during the workout: no rest timer, no weight from last time, and it only knows what you did if you type it.
  • An AI coach appFitbod · Freeletics

    Runs a nice workout and adjusts it.

    Breaks because the plan is theirs, not yours: you can't bring your chat's plan or the AI you chose.
  • IBRA ProThe plan doesn't stay in the chat. It becomes your workout.

    Paste the reply, and IBRA runs it: sets, rest, weights, food and measurements. At the end of the week you share the summary with the chat, and it adjusts.

    Keeps going, because every week the coach who wrote the plan sees what really happened. On iPhone.

4

What to send back, every week.

What turns a good plan into a plan that progresses isn't the first prompt. It's what goes back to the chat every week.

  • Which workouts you completed, by name
  • Your top set for every exercise
  • Weight and waist
  • How many nights you slept under seven hours
  • What was too hard, and what was too easy

And one request: “Send back only what changes, and why.”

That way you approve one or two changes instead of reading a whole new plan every week. That's what the IBRA Pro weekly close does in one share, including photos every three weeks — and you paste the reply back in.

Quick questions

Do I need a paid ChatGPT?

IBRA Pro works with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, even on their free plans. And with no chat at all, IBRA builds the plan itself.

Claude or Gemini instead of ChatGPT?

Yes. A plan is text, and the reply is text. Any chat that can read instructions and return a tidy list works.

How long does the reply take?

For the short prompt, about a minute. For IBRA's full briefing, 2–3 minutes — and the plan is far more precise.
