IBRA or Hevy?
Depends what you need.

Both apps take a plan from ChatGPT. The difference is what happens after the plan.

Hevy fits you if:

  • You already have a plan, and want a great place to log your sets.
  • You want a simple log, without body measurements or a weekly close.

IBRA fits you if:

  • You train at home with your own gear, and haven't seen real change in your body.
  • You want the plan built for that gear, and progressed every week from what you actually did.
  • You want your body measured, not just your sets: waist, shoulders, photos, posture.
  • You want to be told the target for the next set, set by set.

Hevy: a free workout log with a Pro tier; its ChatGPT plugin saves the plan as routines and can review workouts (hevyapp.com, checked November 2026). IBRA: free, with Pro for your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini as coach; on iPhone, and soon on Android. The ranks: Son of Valor, Man of Valor, Mighty Man of Valor.

Can I use both?

Yes. If Hevy is where your history lives, keep it. IBRA is for the part that comes after logging: a plan that changes every week from what you did, and a body that's measured, so you can see if it's working.
