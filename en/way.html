The way
of the hero.

“Who is mighty? The one who masters himself.”
Ethics of the Fathers 4:1

Heroism isn't only the strength of the body. It's the ability to overcome ourselves, keep our word, carry responsibility and choose what's right even when it's hard. We believe every man can grow this kind of heroism, day by day. Not because he's already a hero, but because he chooses to walk the hero's road.

The body is the means. The man is the purpose.

We chose physical training because it lets us practice heroism in everyday life. In every workout we set a goal, meet difficulty, face it and learn to move forward. Along the way we build not only strength and fitness, but discipline, persistence, self-confidence and self-control.

A strong body doesn't necessarily mean good character, but the way we build it can be a chance to build the man we want to be.

Every man. His own way.

Each of us has different goals, abilities and circumstances. That's why we built IBRA: the plan is built for your goals, your time and your gear, and IBRA runs it, measures it and shows your progress.

We don't ask for perfection. The ranks measure the body. Heroism is measured in deeds. We ask you to move forward, keep at it and get stronger, each from his own starting point.

Where it started, and where it's going.

This road began with the people of Israel, in Hebrew. The dream doesn't end there. We hope it reaches men of every nation — men who get stronger not only for themselves, but to carry responsibility, do good, and be a source of strength for their families and their communities. Not from any sense of superiority over others, but from a wish to add good to the world.

Heroism isn't a destination you reach in a day. It's a road you choose again every day.

And the road starts with your next workout.
