To build the hero
in every man.

I dream of a day when every man grows the hero in him. Not only the strength of his body, but the strength of his will, his character, his discipline, his sense of responsibility, and his ability to master himself.

I believe heroism isn't built in one big moment, but in the small things we choose to do, day after day.

I started on this road myself.

I bought gear for my home, because I didn't have two hours for the gym. And still I couldn't bring myself to cancel my membership. Only when I brought AI into my training did I finally cancel it. I got results I'd never had, in limited time and with the gear I had at home. But the real discovery wasn't what I managed to do with my body. It was what this road lets me build inside myself.

I found that training is a wonderful way to practice heroism: set a goal, keep a commitment, keep going when it's hard, and get better over time.

That's where IBRA came from.

I wanted every man to be able to train in a way that's personal, efficient and precise, to see his progress and to enjoy the road.

You don't need to share my outlook to use IBRA. You can simply want to get stronger and see results. But my hope is that along with the body, your persistence, confidence, discipline and self-mastery grow stronger too. That training becomes not just something we do, but a chance to build who we are.

Not just a stronger body — a better man.

A.Y. Lifshitz · founder of IBRA
